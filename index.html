About Music Bot

Music Bot is a free responsive online Facebook Bot (Robot).

It is a handy online bot which can help you find and download your favourite songs while being online. No need to install new apps or navigate to an external website. Just open Facebook Messenger and start chatting with Music Bot.

Send a song title to Music Bot via Facebook and it will reply with a link to the song.
